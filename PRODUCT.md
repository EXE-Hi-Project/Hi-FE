# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hi serves Vietnamese users who want a calmer way to track reproductive health, daily symptoms, cycle context, relationship milestones, and partner care. The app is used in private, often on mobile, when users need quick reassurance, a clear next action, or a small prompt to care for themselves or their partner.

## Product Purpose

Hi helps users understand reproductive health patterns, record meaningful daily context, and coordinate gentle relationship support through dashboards, calendars, notifications, and AI-assisted guidance. Success means users can scan their state quickly, trust the information hierarchy, and complete everyday tasks without feeling judged, overwhelmed, or lost.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, refined.

Hi should feel caring without becoming childish, medically responsible without becoming cold, and romantic without drowning task UI in decoration. The product voice is supportive, concise, and Vietnamese-first.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI SaaS dashboards, neon purple/cyan gradients as a default identity, excessive glassmorphism, decorative gradient text, washed-out gray text on tinted backgrounds, over-rounded nested cards, bouncy motion, and interfaces that feel like greeting cards instead of a trusted health product.

## Design Principles

- Put trust before delight: health and payment surfaces must read as stable, clear, and accountable.
- Make care scannable: dashboards should expose the next useful state, not a decorative pile of cards.
- Use warmth with restraint: pink and romantic accents should guide attention, not coat every component.
- Keep familiar product affordances: buttons, forms, tabs, calendars, drawers, and modals should behave predictably.
- Respect private contexts: copy, layout, and notifications should feel discreet and emotionally safe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for MVP. Body text and labels must have reliable contrast, focus states must be visible, interactive controls must be keyboard reachable, motion must honor reduced-motion preferences, and mobile layouts must avoid clipping, overlap, and tiny touch targets.
